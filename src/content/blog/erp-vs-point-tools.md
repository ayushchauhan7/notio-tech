---
title: "Why growing teams outgrow point tools"
description: "The case for consolidating scattered spreadsheets and single-purpose tools into one SAP-based system of record."
pubDate: 2026-07-02
tags: ["Strategy", "Operations"]
---

Most businesses don't choose their tech stack — they accumulate it. A spreadsheet for budgeting, a separate tool for inventory, another for HR. Each one made sense in isolation.

The cost shows up later: nobody can get a single, current answer to a simple question, because that answer is spread across four systems that don't talk to each other.

Consolidating onto a single ERP isn't about chasing a trend — it's about the moment reconciling data between tools costs more time than the tools save.
