Go-live is usually treated as the finish line. In practice, it's closer to the starting gun for the phase of a SAP engagement that determines whether the investment pays off.

## Stabilise

The first weeks after go-live are about watching closely: ticket volume, performance under real load, and the gap between how a process was designed and how people are actually using it.

## Optimise

Once the system is stable, attention shifts to the backlog of "good enough for now" decisions made during implementation — tightening workflows, automating manual steps, and closing out deferred configuration.

## Evolve

The final phase is ongoing: new modules, regulatory changes, and the slow drift between what the business needs and what the system was originally built for.

Treating these as three distinct phases — rather than one long undifferentiated "support" bucket — is what keeps an SAP landscape from quietly falling behind the business it's meant to serve.
