For businesses new to SAP, the GROW path is designed to shorten the distance between "we should look into SAP" and a running system — pre-configured best-practice processes rather than a blank canvas.

That trade-off is worth understanding before committing: speed and lower upfront cost, in exchange for building change management around the standard process rather than the other way around. For most mid-market teams, that's the right trade. It's worth going in knowing which trade you're making.
